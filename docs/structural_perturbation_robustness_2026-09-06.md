# Structural perturbation robustness — 2026-09-06

## Question

Is the compact six-bit geometric description and its concentrated nonlinear core
specific to the balanced universe `(0,2,4,4)`, or does the same structure
survive nearby selector universes?

## Protocol

The experiment `experiments/structural_perturbation_scan.py` tests 18 universes:

- the balanced universe `(0,2,4,4)`;
- the previously studied robust `(1,0,0,2)` and long `(3,2,4,4)` universes;
- all Hamming-distance-1 parameter changes around the balanced tuple.

For every universe the graph is rebuilt from the same eight public initial
states.  The same semantic coordinate is used without per-universe optimization:

`G = (h1, orientation, phase0, phase1, q0, q1)`.

A canonical binary edge coloring is reconstructed for each operational graph,
and exact ANF forward/reverse laws are solved.

## Results

All 18 universes admitted a binary bidirectionally reversible edge coloring.
The same six-bit coordinate remained injective in all 18 operational graphs.
Most importantly, every one of the 18 universes retained exactly one nonlinear
output channel in the forward direction and exactly one in the reverse direction.

Thus the signature

`five linear channels + one nonlinear channel`

survived 18/18 controlled perturbations.

The nonlinear output indices were also stable across the family:

- forward nonlinear output: coordinate index 4 (`q0`);
- reverse nonlinear output: coordinate index 1 (`orientation`).

The algebraic degree varied by universe.  Examples include degrees 2/2 in the
robust `(1,0,0,2)` universe, 3/4 in the balanced universe, and 4/4 in
`(1,2,4,4)`.

## Dependence on the reversible edge label z

A stronger property was tested: whether nonlinear ANF monomials avoid `z`, so
that the nonlinear correction is generated by the geometric state alone.

This stronger property held in 12 of 18 universes (2/3), including the balanced
universe and the robust `(1,0,0,2)` universe.  It failed in 6 nearby universes,
including the long `(3,2,4,4)` universe.

Therefore:

- concentration into one nonlinear channel per direction is highly robust in
  this perturbation family (18/18);
- independence of that nonlinear core from `z` is robust but not universal
  (12/18).

## Interpretation

The strongest supported invariant is not yet "the nonlinearity never depends on
the data/edge label".  The stronger empirical invariant is:

> within this controlled topological family, reversibility can be represented
> by a fixed minimal six-bit semantic coordinate whose dynamics place all
> irreducible nonlinearity into one output channel in each direction.

The role of `z` is regime-dependent.  In two thirds of tested neighboring
universes it enters only the affine part; in the remaining third it also enters
nonlinear monomials.

This is an automaton-family result, not a physical claim.
